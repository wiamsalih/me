Case study 03 · Fintech

Portfolio Management Dashboard

A client-oriented investment dashboard built around practical portfolio questions, bringing asset allocation, performance attribution, and risk into one view that is easy to scan and explain.

Tools
Tableau
File
Download workbook (opens in a new tab)
01

Problem

Asset allocation, performance, and risk are each complex, and clients need to understand them together to make sense of their portfolio.

02

Users

The dashboard is client-oriented: it is designed for someone reviewing their own investment portfolio.

03

My role

I designed the dashboard logic and structured the visuals around practical investment questions.

04

Key design decisions

Start from client questions, not available data

Each view answers a practical portfolio question, rather than showing every metric the data allows.

One view for allocation, attribution, and risk

Putting the three together makes the portfolio easier to scan, compare, and explain.

Clear information hierarchy

I focused on readability so the dashboard communicates quickly without overwhelming the viewer.
