Key design decisions

Plan meals around shared ingredients

frio plans meals that share ingredients, so food is used before it spoils. The plan says what it achieves, for example “3 dinners, less waste” and “72% of ingredients already planned.”

[ADD: why you chose to show progress this way]

A conversational meal-prep assistant

Instead of a long settings form, the assistant asks about household size, dietary restrictions, and budget.
