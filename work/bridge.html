BridgeMedical Equipment Approval App

A mobile app that maps the insurance approval journey for wheelchairs and medical equipment. It flags stalled steps, explains denials in plain language, and accepts voice and photo input for users with limited mobility.

Problem

Getting insurance approval for a wheelchair or other medical equipment involves many steps, and patients have little visibility into where their request stands.

Our competitive analysis of four existing tools (AuthX, Daiko Health, AuthGuard, and GotDME) found that none gave patients a view of the full process.
